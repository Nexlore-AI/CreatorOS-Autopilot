# Contributor Licence Agreement

> **This is a draft, not legal advice.** It is modelled on the common individual CLA
> pattern (Apache ICLA, the Django/Canonical style). Have a lawyer read it before you ask
> anyone to sign it. Until then, treat contributions as offered under the project's
> licence, [AGPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE).

## Why this exists

CreatorOS Autopilot is licensed under the AGPL-3.0. That licence protects the project: if
someone runs a modified version as a network service, they have to publish their changes.

A hosted version of CreatorOS is planned. For the maintainer to offer that hosted service,
and to dual-licence it, the project needs the right to use contributed code under terms
other than the AGPL. Without a CLA, every past contributor would have to be found and
asked individually before that is possible.

**You keep the copyright in what you write.** This grants a licence; it does not transfer
ownership.
